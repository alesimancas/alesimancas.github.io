Meet

↓

Proton Meet is a privacy first video conferencing product designed for people and teams who need to speak freely online.

The product responds to a growing problem: people are increasingly aware that their online conversations can be accessed, analyzed, logged, or used to train AI systems.

The design challenge was to create a meeting experience that feels familiar enough to use immediately, but different enough to communicate trust, privacy, and control.
