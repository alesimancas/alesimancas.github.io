Hi, I’m ale

I build products where privacy, trust, and digital resilience matter most.

I’m a systems thinker who brings structure and direction to complex product problems. I identify patterns early, connect strategy with execution, and help teams turn ambiguity into clarity.

At Proton, I design privacy first experiences across communication, AI, and Bitcoin self custody. My work connects product strategy, UX, visual direction, and engineering collaboration. Over the past years, I have led design work across Proton Wallet, Proton Meet, Proton Pass, and the next generation of Proton Mail mobile apps, contributing to products used by thousands, and in some cases millions, of people worldwide.

My current focus is digital resilience: helping people and organizations stay in control of their communication, identity, access, information and financial autonomy when the digital world becomes more hostile.

I also care deeply about ethical technology, culture and inclusion. I believe our origins, lived experiences and different perspectives are strengths that can help us build technology with more responsibility, empathy and long term impact.
