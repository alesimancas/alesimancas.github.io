Proton Pass

↓

Proton Pass is an end to end encrypted password manager designed to help people store, share, and autofill credentials securely.

The product challenge was to make account security feel simple and usable without reducing the seriousness of what people are protecting.

Pass was not only about passwords. It was also about helping people protect their identity through email aliases, secure sharing, passkeys, and safer access across devices.
