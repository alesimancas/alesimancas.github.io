Wallet

↓

Proton Wallet is a self custody Bitcoin wallet designed to help people hold and use Bitcoin with more control, privacy, and confidence.

The design challenge was to make a complex and high trust financial product feel approachable without hiding the responsibility that comes with self custody.

The experience needed to support both clarity and confidence: helping users understand what they control, what Proton cannot access, and how to safely manage their Bitcoin.
